## ✨ What Is map2model?  

map2model is a free and simple tool that lets you turn any place on Earth into a 3D model. You open the app, draw a rectangle around any area on a map (like your neighborhood, a park, or a city block), and map2model instantly creates two things for you:  

- A **2D color map** of that area.  
- A **3D white model** of the buildings and terrain.  

You can then export your creation to 17 different file formats, which you can open in popular programs like Blender, AutoCAD, QGIS, or Cesium. It’s like taking a real-world location and turning it into a digital toy you can use in your own projects.

## 🖱️ How to Use map2model  

### 1. Choose Your Area  

When the app opens, you’ll see a map of the world. You can zoom in and out, drag the map to move around, or type a location in the search bar (if available). Find the place you want to turn into a 3D model.  

### 2. Draw a Box  

Look for a button or icon that says "Draw" or "Select Area." Click it, then click and drag on the map to draw a rectangle around the area you want. You can make it as small as a single building or as large as several city blocks.  

### 3. Generate Your Model  

After you draw your box, click the "Generate" or "Create Model" button. The app will process the map data from OpenStreetMap (the free, open-source map service). This may take a few seconds, depending on how large your area is.  

### 4. View Your Results  

Once the process is done, you’ll see two things on your screen:  

- **A 2D color map** – This shows the area with real colors, like a satellite image.  
- **A 3D white model** – This shows the same area as a white, three-dimensional structure. You can rotate it, zoom in, and look at it from different angles.

## 📦 Exporting Your Model  

This is where map2model shines. You can export your 3D model to **17 different file formats**. Here are the most common ones and what they’re used for:  

| Format | What It’s Used For |  
|--------|--------------------|  
| **GLB** | 3D models for web, games, and augmented reality |  
| **OBJ** | Universal 3D format, works with almost any 3D software |  
| **DXF** | AutoCAD and other CAD programs |  
| **CityJSON** | Urban planning and GIS applications |  
| **3D Tiles** | For large 3D maps in web apps like Cesium |  
| **Blender files** | Directly open in Blender for editing |  

To export, simply click the "Export" button and choose the format you want. The file will download to your computer. Then you can open it in the program of your choice.

## 💻 What’s Under the Hood?  

If you’re curious (but you don’t need to know this to use the app), map2model is built with:  

- **Vue3** – A modern web framework that makes the interface fast and responsive.  
- **FastAPI** – A Python backend that handles all the heavy lifting.  
- **OpenStreetMap** – The free map data source that powers everything.  
- **Three.js** – The 3D engine that renders your models in the browser.  
- **MapLibre** – The map viewer you use to draw your box.  

All of this runs locally on your machine, so your data stays private.
